The Journey

Years of Struggle & Resilience

Every success story has chapters of pain. Here's what nobody talks about.

1989

Born in Karachi

Born into a lower middle-class family in Karachi, Pakistan. From the very beginning, financial constraints shaped his worldview. While other children dreamed of toys, he dreamed of opportunities his family couldn't afford.

2007-2010

Engineering Dreams Deferred

Enrolled in Mechanical Engineering with hopes of a stable career. But the financial burden became unbearable. Watching his parents struggle to pay fees, he made the hardest decision: dropping out to support his family.

2010

America or Nothing

With empty pockets but a heart full of dreams, he moved to the United States. No safety net, no connections, no guarantee. Just a suitcase and an unshakeable belief that things would be different.

2010-2013

The Darkest Years

Visa rejections. Financial crisis. Psychological pressure. Working multiple low-paying jobs just to survive. Sleeping in cramped apartments. Eating the cheapest food. Every day was a battle between giving up and pushing forward.

2013-2015

First Breakthrough

Started small retail cellphone business. 16-hour workdays. Learning everything from scratch. Making mistakes. Losing money. Getting back up. The first taste of success came, but it was built on years of invisible struggle.

2015-2020

Building an Empire

Expanded into real estate. Multi-million dollar ventures. But success brought new challenges: criticism, accusations, people questioning his methods. The same people who ignored him during struggle now scrutinized his success.

2020-Present

Giving Back

Founded TheHUniversity and TradingPapa. Not for profit, but for purpose. Investing $100,000+ of his own money to provide free education. Facing accusations of being a "scammer" while working tirelessly to prove his mission is real.
